Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary

Your charts, editing history, settings and preview assets are stored in your browser. Clearing this site’s data deletes these local copies. The editor does not require an account or use cookies.

Usage statistics are on by default unless your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. You can turn them on or off for this browser in Settings › About › Send Usage Statistics. If you change this setting, it takes precedence over the browser signals.

Data Controller

%PRODUCT_NAME% is an open-source project run by qwewqa, the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. “We” and “us” refer to qwewqa.

Send privacy questions and requests to nse@qwewqa.dev.

Usage Statistics

We collect usage statistics because we have a legitimate interest in understanding how the editor is used and improving it.

The statistics include:

  • Tools and commands you use, some editor settings and time spent in the editor.
  • How long actions take, other performance measurements and the number of errors.
  • File types and sizes, chart sizes (such as the number of notes), and the length of the music.
  • Results of attempts to open, save or export files.
  • Browser and device details, language settings, screen size, your country and the site that referred you.
  • Whether browser storage is available, how much is used and its limits.

The statistics do not include chart content, file names, level titles or other text you type. For the full list, see What the usage statistics send.

Counting Visits

We count visits using a visitor identifier generated from your IP address and browser details. It changes each day and is stored with individual usage records. We also use your IP address to estimate your country. The statistics do not contain your IP address, but error logs on the statistics server may contain it.

Hosting and Sharing

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log requests, including your IP address, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

We store usage statistics and their backups with Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States. AWS processes this data on our behalf. We do not share usage statistics with anyone else.

Our email provider processes the messages and attachments you send us.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Individual usage records, including visitor identifiers: up to 13 months.
  • Backups of the statistics: up to six weeks after each backup is made.
  • Error logs on the statistics server: up to two weeks.
  • Email conversations: up to one year after your last message.

Aggregated statistics contain no visitor identifiers and are kept indefinitely.

Email and Support

When you email us, we use your email address and message to respond, based on our legitimate interest in providing support. For requests about your data protection rights, we use your message to respond as required by law.

If you send a file to help us investigate a problem, we use it only for that and delete it once the problem is fixed.

Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, receive a copy in a reusable format, or restrict how we use it.

You can object at any time to our use of your personal data for usage statistics or support. You can also turn off usage statistics in Settings › About › Send Usage Statistics.

To exercise any of these rights, email nse@qwewqa.dev. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

For usage statistics, we may not be able to find the records that belong to you.

Changes

We may update this notice as the editor changes.
