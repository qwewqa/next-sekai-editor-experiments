Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Your Data and Choices

Your charts, editing history, settings and stored assets are kept in this browser’s storage. Clearing the site’s data in your browser deletes this stored data. The web editor works without accounts or cookies.

Usage statistics are optional. You can turn them off for this browser in Settings › About › Send Usage Statistics.

Statistics are on by default unless your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Your choice overrides these browser signals.

Who Runs This

%PRODUCT_NAME% is an open-source project by qwewqa. qwewqa runs this site and is responsible for the use of personal data described here.

Send privacy questions and requests to nse@qwewqa.dev.

Usage Statistics

We collect usage statistics to understand how the editor is used and improve its reliability and performance. We rely on our legitimate interest in improving the editor.

  • Use of tools and commands, settings, time spent in the editor, performance and errors.
  • File types and sizes, chart sizes, music lengths and the results of file actions.
  • Browser and device information, browser storage status, country and referring site.

Statistics do not include chart content, file names, level titles or other text you type.

See the complete list of what is sent.

Counting Visits

To count visits, the server uses your IP address and information about your browser to create a daily visitor code. The code is stored with your activity and changes each day.

Where Data Is Processed

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may log requests, including your IP address, and keeps those logs under its own privacy statement.

We process and store usage statistics on Amazon Web Services (AWS) servers in the United States. AWS provides hosting and backups and is the only provider with whom we share these statistics.

For transfers subject to EU data protection rules, we rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in AWS’s terms.

How Long We Keep Data

We keep individual usage statistics for 13 months.

We keep combined statistics without visitor codes indefinitely.

Backups on AWS are deleted after about five weeks.

Server error logs may contain your IP address. We delete these logs after two weeks.

Email and Files You Send Us

When you contact us, we use your email address and message to answer you. We rely on our legitimate interest in providing support. For requests about your data rights, we use this information to meet our legal obligations. We keep the conversation in our mailbox for one year after your last message, so we can refer to it if you contact us again. If you send a file to help fix a problem, we use it only for that and delete it when the problem is fixed.

Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, receive a portable copy, restrict its use, or object to its use. Send requests to the email address above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

You can object at any time to our use of your personal data for usage statistics or support by emailing us. You can also stop sending usage statistics in Settings › About › Send Usage Statistics.

We may be unable to identify which usage statistics relate to you.

Changes

We may update this notice as the editor changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
