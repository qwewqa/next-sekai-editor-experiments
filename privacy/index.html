Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026

At a Glance

Your charts, editing history, settings and assets are stored in your browser. Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes that local data. The editor works without accounts or cookies.

Usage statistics are on by default unless your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. You can turn them on or off for this browser in Settings › About › Send Usage Statistics. The editor follows your choice even if your browser sends one of these signals.

Data Controller

%PRODUCT_NAME% is an open-source project run by qwewqa, the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. “We” and “us” refer to qwewqa.

Send privacy questions and requests to nse@qwewqa.dev.

Usage Statistics

We collect usage statistics to understand how the editor is used, based on our legitimate interest in improving its reliability and performance.

The statistics include:

  • Which tools and commands you use, selected settings and time spent in the editor.
  • Performance measurements, such as how long actions take, and counts of errors.
  • File types and sizes, chart sizes such as the number of notes, the duration of music, and the outcomes of opening, saving and exporting files.
  • Browser and device details, language settings, screen size, your country and the site that referred you, together with information about the availability, use and limits of browser storage.

Statistics do not include chart content, file names, level titles or other text you type. The usage statistics reference lists every field the editor sends.

Counting Visits

To count visits, we use your IP address and browser details to generate a visitor identifier for each day. We store the identifier with your usage records. Your IP address is not stored with the statistics.

Hosting and Sharing

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may log requests, including your IP address. Its privacy statement explains how it handles this information.

Our statistics server and backups of the statistics are hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States. AWS processes this data on our behalf. We do not share usage statistics with anyone else.

For transfers to AWS that are subject to EU data protection rules, we use the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses in AWS’s terms.

Our email provider processes the messages and attachments you send us.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Usage records, including visitor identifiers: up to 13 months.
  • Backups of the statistics: up to six weeks from creation.
  • Error logs on the statistics server: up to two weeks. These may contain your IP address.
  • Email conversations: up to one year after your last message.

Combined statistics contain no visitor identifiers and are kept indefinitely.

Contacting Us

When you email us, we use your address and message to respond, based on our legitimate interest in providing support. For requests to exercise your data protection rights, we process your request to meet our legal obligations.

If you send a file to help fix a problem, we use it only for that purpose and delete it once the problem is fixed.

Your Rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or receive a copy in a reusable format. You may also have the right to restrict or object to our use of your data. To make a request, email nse@qwewqa.dev. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

For requests about usage statistics, we may not be able to identify your records because the visitor identifier is different each day.

You can object at any time to our use of your personal data for usage statistics or support. To object, email nse@qwewqa.dev. You can also turn off usage statistics in Settings › About › Send Usage Statistics.

Changes

We may update this notice as the editor changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
